Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

MIC (“we”, “us”) helps you get trusted recommendations from the people you know — your circles, your contacts and local businesses. This policy explains what information the MIC app collects, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us

  • Account details — your phone number (used to sign in and verify your account), your name and, if you add them, your email address, profile photo and address.
  • Circles and posts — circles you create or join, members you add, recommendations, ratings and reviews, comments, service requests and anything else you post.
  • Messages — the content of chats you send to other people and to your circles.
  • Business profiles — if you create a business: its name, description, category, address and map location, services, prices, availability, portfolio photos and verification details.
  • Bookings — services you book or receive bookings for, including the time, price and any notes added to the booking.
  • Photos — images you choose from your library or take with your camera, for example your profile photo, circle images and business portfolio.

2. Information from your device

  • Contacts (with your permission) — if you allow access, the names and phone numbers in your address book are uploaded to our servers so we can show which of your contacts use MIC and help you build your circles and invite people. You can decline, and you can turn access off at any time in your device settings.
  • Camera and photo library (with your permission) — used only when you choose to take or select a photo.
  • Notifications — if you allow notifications, we store a push-notification token for your device so we can tell you about messages, bookings, circle invites and other activity.
  • Search history — searches you make in the app are saved so you can see and reuse your recent searches. You can remove them from your search history in the app.
  • Data stored on your device — to keep you signed in and make the app faster, MIC keeps your sign-in tokens in your device’s secure storage and caches some information (such as your profile and recent chat previews) on your device. This is removed when you sign out.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To run the app’s features: circles, recommendations, requests, search, chat, business profiles and bookings.
  • To show which of your contacts are on MIC and help you connect with them.
  • To send you notifications you’ve allowed about activity relevant to you.
  • To keep MIC safe — preventing spam, abuse and fraud, and handling reports and blocks.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to show you third-party advertising.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other MIC users — your name and profile photo, and what you post, are visible to the people and circles you share them with. Open circles and business profiles can be seen more widely within MIC.
  • Businesses you book — when you book a service, the business sees your name, profile photo, the booking details and any notes you add.
  • Service providers — companies that help us run MIC, such as our hosting and storage providers and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. They process information only on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of MIC, our users or others.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide MIC. You can edit your profile, delete posts and remove photos in the app.

To delete your account and the information associated with it, email us at privacy@example.com from the details linked to your account. We will delete or anonymise your information unless we need to keep some of it to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent abuse.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Turn contacts, camera, photo and notification permissions on or off at any time in your device settings.
  • Change your notification preferences in the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to how we use it. Contact us to make a request.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and secure storage of sign-in tokens on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

MIC is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your information, email privacy@example.com.
